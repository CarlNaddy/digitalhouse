## Purpose

Defines each product's price as a seeded, non-linear path over time: exactly $1.00 at the product's creation, drifting upward over the years with multi-timescale rallies and drawdowns, and fully reproducible for any past or future instant from just the product's seed and creation date.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Price is a pure function of seed, creation time, and the query time

A product's price at any instant `t` SHALL be computed solely from the product's `PriceSeed`, its `CreatedAt`, and `t`. It SHALL NOT depend on any other stored field, on configuration, on trading history, or on any prior price. The same `(PriceSeed, CreatedAt, t)` SHALL always yield the same price, in every process and at any later date.

#### Scenario: Same inputs, same price

- **WHEN** the price is evaluated twice for the same product and the same `t`
- **THEN** both evaluations return the identical `long` micro-USD value

#### Scenario: Unrelated changes do not move the historical curve

- **WHEN** a product's title, slug, retirement state, ownership, or resale listing changes
- **THEN** its price at every past `t` is exactly what it was before the change

#### Scenario: Deterministic across processes

- **WHEN** the price for a given `(PriceSeed, CreatedAt, t)` is computed in two separate processes
- **THEN** both produce the same value

### Requirement: Every product is worth exactly $1.00 at creation

At `t = CreatedAt` the price SHALL be exactly `1_000_000` micro-USD ($1.00), for every `PriceSeed`.

#### Scenario: Anchor

- **WHEN** the price is evaluated at `t = CreatedAt`
- **THEN** it is exactly `1_000_000` micro-USD

#### Scenario: Before creation clamps to the anchor

- **WHEN** the price is evaluated at a `t` earlier than `CreatedAt`
- **THEN** it is `1_000_000` micro-USD (age is treated as zero)

### Requirement: The path is non-linear with multi-timescale movement

The price path SHALL exhibit oscillations at several distinct timescales (from multi-year down to sub-monthly) superimposed on the drift, so that it is not a straight line and not a smooth monotonic curve. Different `PriceSeed` values SHALL produce visibly different paths.

#### Scenario: Not a straight line

- **WHEN** a product's log-price is sampled at regular intervals over several years
- **THEN** the second differences of the samples are not all approximately equal (the path curves and reverses, it does not merely slope)

#### Scenario: Seeds differ

- **WHEN** two products share a `CreatedAt` but have different `PriceSeed`s
- **THEN** their prices at the same set of sample times differ materially

### Requirement: Long-run upward drift

Over long horizons the price SHALL trend upward: the average price across samples taken far from creation SHALL exceed the average across samples taken near creation. A product created years earlier SHALL, on average, be worth more today than one created recently.

#### Scenario: Older is pricier on average

- **WHEN** the price is averaged over many sample times in the product's tenth year and over many sample times in its first year
- **THEN** the tenth-year average is greater than the first-year average

#### Scenario: Price stays positive

- **WHEN** the price is evaluated at any `t`
- **THEN** it is greater than zero (a deep trough may take a young product below $1.00, which is permitted)

### Requirement: `PriceSeed` and `CreatedAt` are immutable

Once a product exists, its `PriceSeed` and `CreatedAt` SHALL NOT change. Neither is settable through any form model after creation, and a forced write to either on an already-persisted product SHALL be rejected by the persistence layer. `PriceSeed` is generated by the system at creation; `CreatedAt` is chosen at creation (defaulting to the present, backdatable, never in the future).

#### Scenario: Seed cannot change

- **WHEN** code modifies a persisted product's `PriceSeed` and saves
- **THEN** the save is rejected and the value is unchanged

#### Scenario: Creation date cannot change

- **WHEN** code modifies a persisted product's `CreatedAt` and saves
- **THEN** the save is rejected and the value is unchanged

#### Scenario: Admin sets the issued date on create

- **WHEN** an admin creates a product with an issued date of 2015-06-01
- **THEN** the product's `CreatedAt` is 2015-06-01 and its price today reflects roughly a decade of drift and oscillation

#### Scenario: Future issued date rejected

- **WHEN** an admin submits an issued date later than today
- **THEN** the form is rejected and no product is created

### Requirement: Trading and admin actions never alter the curve

Buying, reselling, marketplace buyback, retirement, un-retirement, and any admin edit of a product's metadata SHALL leave the price at every `t` unchanged. There is no operation that re-bases, re-rolls, or shifts a product's price path.

#### Scenario: Full lifecycle leaves the curve intact

- **WHEN** a product is bought, resold, bought back, retired, un-retired, and has its title and slug edited
- **THEN** its price at any chosen past `t` is identical before and after the whole sequence

### Requirement: The current price is a cache of the path at "now"

The system SHALL persist each product's current price as `PriceAt(product, now)` and refresh it on the scheduled recompute cadence (a recurring background job), appending a `Scheduled` price-history snapshot each time. Missed or repeated recompute runs SHALL NOT corrupt the value, since it is recomputed from the path.

#### Scenario: Recompute matches the path

- **WHEN** the scheduled recompute runs for a product
- **THEN** its stored current price equals `PriceAt(product, now)` and a `Scheduled` snapshot with that value is recorded

#### Scenario: Snapshots are the only kind

- **WHEN** any price-history snapshot is written
- **THEN** its cause is `Scheduled` (there is no admin price adjustment in this design)

### Requirement: Trailing 12-month growth

The system SHALL expose, per product, `PriceAt(now) − PriceAt(now − 1 year)` in USD, using the path for both evaluations. For a product younger than a year the earlier point is its creation ($1.00).

#### Scenario: Growth for an older product

- **WHEN** a product created several years ago is viewed
- **THEN** its "growth last year" equals its price now minus its price one year ago, which may be positive or negative

### Requirement: Price-history chart with a selectable time range

The product detail page SHALL let the viewer choose the chart's time range from at least: 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 1 year, and All (since creation). The chart SHALL sample the path at a resolution appropriate to the chosen range, SHALL never plot a point earlier than `CreatedAt`, and SHALL default to a range that shows meaningful history (1 year, or All when the product is younger than a year).

#### Scenario: Switching range re-samples

- **WHEN** the viewer switches the range from 1 year to 24 hours
- **THEN** the chart redraws with closely-spaced intraday points spanning the last 24 hours

#### Scenario: Range clamps to creation

- **WHEN** the viewer selects a range longer than the product's age (e.g. 1 year on a 2-month-old product)
- **THEN** the earliest plotted point is the product's creation, not a time before it

#### Scenario: Young product default

- **WHEN** a product younger than one year is opened
- **THEN** the chart defaults to the "All" range

### Requirement: Chart hover readout

While the pointer is over the chart, the system SHALL indicate the price at the pointer's position on the time axis — a guide on the hovered point plus a readout of that point's date and price.

#### Scenario: Hover shows a value

- **WHEN** the viewer moves the pointer across the chart
- **THEN** a marker follows the line and a label shows the date and price at that position
